PHASE 13B – PREMIUM INVOICE LOGO + ACTIVATION EMAIL

Changes:
1. Worker invoice PDF keeps the selected Editorial Premium design and now uses a cleaner invoice logo without the beige square background.
   - logo rendered slightly larger
   - visually closer to the earlier premium mockup
   - QR area kept unchanged functionally

2. Automatic activation email added after the 14-day withdrawal waiting period.
   - the scheduled Worker now checks whether a package has become fully/partially available
   - if needed, a one-time email is sent automatically
   - depending on checkout consents, the email says either:
       - package fully unlocked
       - appointments are now bookable
       - materials are now available
   - the system generates a fresh access token for that email so the customer receives a working personal access link

3. Database updated with activation_email_sent_at on invoices to ensure the activation email is sent only once.
