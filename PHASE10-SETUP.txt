PHASE 10 – PACKAGE IDENTITY FIX

Main bug fixed:
When one customer owned multiple packages of the same session type, the Worker picked the first eligible credit row.
That is why booking from Package BD-P000002 could reduce BD-P000001.

Now:
- each package card passes its exact credit_id into the booking flow
- the Worker validates and consumes only that exact package credit
- two Poly Starter packages with 3 sessions each will correctly become 2/3 and 2/3 after one booking from each
