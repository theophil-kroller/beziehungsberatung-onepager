BUILD 8 FIX3 — Stripe single-session invoice recovery & paid invoice layout

Fixes after the first successful Stripe appointment payment test:

1. Single-session invoices no longer write invoice_items with purchase_id=NULL.
   A proper purchase row is created for session-individual / session-couple and linked to the invoice item.

2. Recovery from partial Stripe booking finalization.
   If Google Calendar and the booking confirmation were already created but invoice creation failed,
   the Worker now detects the existing booking by Stripe session ID, repairs/reuses the incomplete invoice,
   and finalizes the checkout without duplicating the appointment or triggering a refund.

3. /stripe/checkout-status retries paid Stripe sessions that previously ended in status=error,
   enabling the existing failed return page to self-heal after deploying this fix.

4. Paid invoice format improved:
   - service name (e.g. Einzelberatung)
   - quantity
   - unit price
   - amount
   - 'Betrag dankend erhalten'
   - payment method (e.g. Online-Zahlung über Stripe)
   - payment date
   - 'BEZAHLT AM' instead of a due date
   - no bank details or payment QR on paid invoices

5. Paid appointment invoice email now uses 'Zahlung erhalten' wording and attaches the paid invoice PDF.

Deploy only cloudflare-worker/worker.js to apply the runtime fix. No new D1 migration is required for FIX3.
