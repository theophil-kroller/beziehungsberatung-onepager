BUILD 11 LOCAL AI TEST CASE — MARA & JONAS
===========================================

Use only fictional data for the first model-quality test.

Context
-------
Mara, 34, Jonas, 37, together for 8 years, open relationship for about a year.
Goal: create more security for Mara without Jonas feeling controlled.

Prior notes
-----------
Session 1: jealousy especially around spontaneous dates. Distinguished security needs
from controlling behaviour. Homework: notice triggers.

Session 2: late information was identified as part of a cycle. Agreement: announce
dates ideally 48h in advance, no obligation to report intimate details.

Session 3: 48h rule helped. Conflict about unexpectedly late return. Agreement: no
fixed curfew; send a short message when expected timing changes. Overnight stays left open.

Raw notes for Session 4
-----------------------
Mara says week was mostly good. Friday problem: Jonas had a date with Lea, announced
Tuesday so rule was followed. Around 00:30 he wrote it would be later. Mara still felt
strongly tense, checked Lea's Instagram and checked her phone many times. Jonas was
annoyed when he came home because Mara immediately asked whether they had sex.

Jonas says he feels like whatever he does is never enough. Mara says when information
is missing she imagines things. Asked what she actually needs to know to feel safe
enough: that the relationship is not suddenly at risk and Jonas is still "with her";
not necessarily sexual details.

Jonas could send a short message after a date (on the way home / arrived) but does not
want constant updates during the date. Mara thinks that may be enough.

Overnight stays discussed. Mara currently says clearly no. Jonas says maybe in the
future but not urgently. Mara fears the relationship will keep opening further. Jonas
says he does not want to keep pushing boundaries but also does not want permanent bans.

Intervention: do not decide the entire future today; use temporary agreements instead
of permanent rules. Safety scale Mara 5/10, three weeks ago 3/10. Jonas satisfaction
with autonomy 7/10.

Agreement: no overnight stays for four weeks, then review. Jonas sends a brief message
when on his way home or when he arrives. No obligation to report sexual details.

At the end Mara visibly calmer. Jonas says the four-week rule feels okay.
Possible next focus: what security means for Mara beyond rules; how Jonas can reassure
without experiencing control. Package has one session remaining; possibly discuss continuation.

Quality checks
--------------
- should NOT diagnose Mara
- should NOT import old sleep problems into Session 4
- should distinguish client statements from observations
- should identify temporary agreement and next focus
- should keep commercial/package note separate from clinical interpretation
